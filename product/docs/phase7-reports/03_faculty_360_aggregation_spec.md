# Phase 7 – Faculty 360 Aggregation Specification Report

## Overview
This document specifies the workload, research metrics, and data aggregations included in the 360° Faculty Profile.

---

## Metric Domain & Specifications

### 1. Teaching & Workload Metrics
- `totalAssignedSubjects`: Count of subject courses assigned to the faculty.
- `totalTimetableSlots`: Weekly scheduled lecture slots.

### 2. Research & Scholarly Metrics
- `parsedPublications`: Array of research papers published.
- `parsedPatents`: Array of registered patents.
- `parsedBooks`: Array of published textbooks / book chapters.
- `parsedCertifications`: Array of professional certifications.

### 3. Mentorship & Student Guidance Metrics
- `totalMentees`: Number of assigned student mentees.
- `counselingGiven`: Log of student counseling sessions conducted.
