## Overview

## Metric Formulas
$$\text{Attendance Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Present} + \text{OnDuty} + \text{Excused}}{\text{Total Sessions}} \right) \times 100$$
$$\text{Pass Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Passed}}{\text{Appeared}} \right) \times 100$$
